How RentShield checks your listing

RentShield combines 5 transparent local verification layers to calculate an actionable risk score.

1

Price

Checks whether the rent looks unusual compared to the median rates in our 30+ neighborhood statistical matrix.

2

Images

Checks for possible reused or suspicious images, compression artifacts, and mobile screenshot aspect ratios.

3

Description

Checks for copied or unusual descriptions and matches text against known fraud syndicate scripts.

4

Payment

Checks for suspicious payment requests such as gate passes, urgent UPI QR transfers, or pre-visit deposits.

5

Location

Checks whether the property details, stated landmarks, and pin code formatting are consistent.

RentShield combines these signals to create a risk score.

Do not assume the score is an absolute guarantee. Use it as an educated indicator to guide your physical inspection.
